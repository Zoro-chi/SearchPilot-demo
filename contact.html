What happens next

Three steps, no drip campaign. If we are not the right fit for what you describe, we will say so in the first reply and point you somewhere better.

  1. We read it properly

    Your note is read by the team that would run the engagement, not by a routing inbox, and we check it against what we have seen on comparable accounts.

  2. You hear back quickly

    A named engineer replies within one business day, and if the question needs a data specialist, they join the thread rather than bouncing it elsewhere.

  3. We walk through your site

    We run the session, share the notes, and send a written summary with the changes we would prioritise first and how we would measure them.
