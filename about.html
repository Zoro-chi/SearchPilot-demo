About

We build the reporting layer we wanted as growth teams

Northline started inside a B2B marketing team that could see clicks but never revenue. We built the tool we kept asking analytics for, and we still run it the same way.

How we started

Northline began in 2021 when three of us were running demand generation for a mid-market software company. We had a warehouse full of impressions and a spreadsheet nobody trusted, so every weekly review opened with an argument about whose numbers were right. The first version of Northline was a script that reconciled search visibility against pipeline data and ranked pages by the revenue they were worth.

The first eighteen months

We spent that time with customers who did not want another dashboard. They wanted to know which three pages were worth a week of engineering time. That question shaped everything: the evidence model, the way recommendations are ranked, and the review workflow that keeps a human accountable for what actually ships.

How we work

Evidence over opinion

Every recommendation we publish points at something real: a query, a page, a metric, a date range. If we cannot show the evidence, we do not publish the recommendation. That rule makes us slower than tools that generate advice from a model, and considerably faster at the work that changes a ranking. Our engagements are built around the same principle.

Leadership

We are fourteen people across engineering, data, and customer strategy. Half of us have run marketing teams; the rest have spent their careers building the analytics those teams depend on. Between us we have shipped three migrations off a legacy tag manager, and we are not shy about mentioning it.

Working with us

Engagements start with a working session, not a contract. We look at the pages you already care about, the reports you already run, and the decisions your team finds hardest. From there we agree what a good outcome looks like and write it down. If you would rather see how the work runs before committing, get in touch and we will walk you through a real example.
