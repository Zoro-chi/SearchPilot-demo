How Northline works

We connect the tools you already run, read the evidence behind every recommendation, and hand your team work that is ordered by impact instead of by who noticed it first.

  1. Connect your sources

    Point Northline at your analytics suite, your search console, and your CRM. The connection is read-only, takes an afternoon, and never moves customer data between systems.

  2. Read the evidence

    Every suggestion arrives with the query, page, and metric that produced it. Teams can check the reasoning instead of trusting a score that appeared from nowhere.

  3. Ship in priority order

    Work is grouped into sprints with owners, effort estimates, and expected movement. When a release lands, Northline re-measures and updates the ranking automatically.

What teams get

The reports replace the weekly scramble of screenshots and spreadsheets with one source of truth that marketing, product, and engineering can argue with.

  • A prioritised backlog of technical and content fixes, each scored against pipeline rather than impressions.
  • Coverage maps that show which revenue topics your site owns, and where a competitor owns them instead.
  • Weekly change summaries that translate releases into visible movement for leadership.
  • Auditable history for every recommendation, including what was approved, edited, or rejected and why.
  • Exports for your warehouse, so reporting lives alongside the rest of your metrics.

Built for teams that have to justify the work

Most teams already know their site has problems. The hard part is deciding what to fix first, explaining the choice, and proving it mattered. Northline removes the guesswork from that conversation by keeping the evidence attached to the work.
